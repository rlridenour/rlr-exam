* Short Essay

1. Explain, in your own words, the relationship between mitosis and cell growth. Your answer should mention chromosomes and cell division.
   :PROPERTIES:
   :SPACE: 2.5in
   :END:
   :ANSWER:
   Mitosis is the process of nuclear division that produces two genetically
   identical daughter cells. It allows an organism to grow by increasing its
   number of cells, replacing damaged or worn-out cells, and enabling asexual
   reproduction in some organisms. Each daughter cell receives a full,
   identical set of chromosomes.
   :END:

2. Compare and contrast /prokaryotic/ and *eukaryotic* cells, giving at least two structural differences.
   :PROPERTIES:
   :SPACE: 3.5in
   :END:
   :ANSWER:
   Eukaryotic cells have a membrane-bound nucleus and other membrane-bound
   organelles (e.g. mitochondria, ~ER~), while prokaryotic cells lack a
   nucleus and these organelles, with DNA free in the cytoplasm.
   Eukaryotic cells are also generally larger than prokaryotic cells.
   :END:
